Exact Physical Pin Order for 30-Pin DOIT ESP32 DevKit V1 + 16-Pin TB6612FNG + HC-SR04 Voltage Divider + Dual Power Supplies

1. NEVER Touch 6V Battery (VM) to ESP32

The 4×AA Battery Red (+) wire goes ONLY to TB6612 VM (via switch). TB6612 logic VCC gets 3.3V from ESP32 3V3. Never short VM and VCC together!

2. ALWAYS Use 1kΩ + 2kΩ on HC-SR04 ECHO

HC-SR04 runs at 5V (from ESP32 VIN), so its ECHO pin outputs 5V. Passing it through 1kΩ series + 2kΩ to GND steps it down to a safe 3.33V for ESP32 D35.

3. Common Ground (GND) Ties All 4 Together

ESP32 GND, TB6612 GND, 4×AA Battery Black (-), and HC-SR04 GND must all plug into the same Breadboard Ground Rail.
